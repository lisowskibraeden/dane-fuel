Estimate calculation ⌄

Wisconsin DATCP’s standard retail calculation uses the greater of eligible invoice/replacement cost plus 6% and eligible terminal-based cost plus 9.18%. Costs include applicable taxes, transportation and other required adjustments. The headline runs both branches using the latest terminal quote as an assumed invoice/replacement cost and eligible terminal cost, with an illustrative 2.5¢ delivery charge. These costs and dates are proxies, not verified retailer inputs.
