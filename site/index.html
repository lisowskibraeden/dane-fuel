Minimum-price inputs ⌄

All costs below are dollars per gallon, including applicable taxes, delivery and other charges, after required trade-discount adjustments. Use the closest terminal and the exact fuel product.

The standard calculation takes the greater of the eligible terminal-based cost plus 9.18% and the eligible invoice/replacement cost plus the applicable markup. It is not a single countywide legal price. Competition-matching and other exceptions are not modeled.
