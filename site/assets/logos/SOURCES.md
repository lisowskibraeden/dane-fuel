# Station brand identifiers

Logos are unmodified brand marks displayed solely to identify station brands; no affiliation is implied. Local files avoid a dependency on paid image services.

- Kwik Trip: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kwik_Trip_Logo.svg (PD-textlogo; trademark rights retained).
- Costco: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Costco_Wholesale_logo_2010-10-26.svg (PD-textlogo; trademark rights retained).
- Woodman's: https://www.woodmans-food.com/brandresources/ (standard employee-owned logo).
- CITGO: https://www.citgo.com/news-and-media/corporate-logos-and-standards/ (official color mark).
- Amoco: https://www.bp.com/en/global/corporate/who-we-are/our-brands.html (Amoco brand card).

Unknown brands use a neutral initial until a sourced logo is added.
